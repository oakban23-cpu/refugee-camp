# REFUGEE: emote sticker set (14 stickers on 1 sheet)

This is another small job for **REFUGEE (ค่ายผู้อพยพ)**, the fantasy RPG for phones you have been painting for. Thank you for the world map and the medallions; they are in the game and look amazing.

## What it is for
Players use **emotes** to talk to each other: waving, laughing, crying, sending love, and so on. Right now the emote menu and the little speech bubble over a character's head use **the phone's own emoji** (`refs/current_emote_menu.png`). They look different on every phone, and they don't match the game.

Please paint **one matching sticker set** to replace them. The game shows each sticker in two places:
- in the **emote menu**, at about **28–34 px**;
- in a small **speech bubble above the character's head**, at about **26–30 px**, while the character plays the animation.

So each sticker must read instantly at a tiny size.

## Style
- Make the stickers **cute chibi game stickers** that fit the in-game characters (`refs/game_chibi_characters.jpg`): big-headed, big shiny eyes, rosy cheeks, bold dark outlines, soft shading and warm bright colours. Give them the same painted quality as our UI icons (`refs/approved_style_painted_ui_icons.jpg`), but simpler and bolder, like a messaging-app sticker pack.
- **Use one mascot in all the face stickers:** a small, round, gender-neutral chibi head with warm peach skin, big sparkling dark-brown eyes, rosy cheeks and a little tuft of chestnut hair. Show the same face in every sticker, with only the expression and the hands changing, so the 14 stickers look like one set. Hands use the same skin tone.
- **One idea per sticker**, with a strong silhouette and few details. Expressions should be **exaggerated**, like a manga reaction.
- **No text, letters or numbers.** Symbols are fine: music notes, hearts, sparkles, an anger mark, tears, a small sleep bubble.
- Every sticker must have a **clearly different silhouette and main colour**.

## Format (please follow exactly)
- **One sheet: `emotes.png`, 1024×1024**, a **4 × 4 grid** of exactly **256×256** cells with no gutters (`templates/template_emotes_4x4.png`). Cells are numbered left to right, top to bottom.
- **Cells 1–14** hold the stickers. **Cells 15 and 16 are (empty)**: leave them plain black.
- Each sticker is centred in its cell and **fills about 85%** of it, with **at least 12 px of empty space** to every cell edge. Nothing may touch or cross into the next cell.
- Each sticker has a **thick white die-cut sticker outline** (about 6–8 px) all the way around its silhouette.
- **Everything outside the white outline is flat pure black (#000000).** No shadow, glow or gradient outside the outline. The game cuts the stickers out along the white outline.

## The 14 stickers
1. **Wave:** the mascot smiling brightly, one hand raised and waving, with small motion arcs beside the hand.
2. **Dance:** the mascot dancing happily, eyes closed with joy, one arm up, with two colourful music notes floating around.
3. **Rest by the fire:** the mascot sitting cosily, sleepy and content with half-closed eyes, next to a tiny warm campfire, with a small sleep bubble. Do not use a "Z" letter.
4. **Bow:** the mascot bowing politely with eyes closed, head tilted down and hands together in front, with a small sparkle of respect.
5. **Laugh:** the mascot laughing so hard that tears of joy fly out, mouth wide open, squeezed-shut eyes.
6. **Send love:** the mascot happily hugging or blowing a **big glossy red heart**, with small hearts floating.
7. **Cheer:** the mascot with both arms up, cheering, next to a party popper bursting colourful confetti.
8. **Thumbs up:** the mascot winking with a confident grin and a big thumbs-up, with a sparkle.
9. **Cry:** the mascot bawling, with two fountain streams of tears and a wobbly mouth.
10. **Angry:** the mascot fuming, with puffed red cheeks, angry brows, a **red anger-vein mark** on the head and a puff of steam.
11. **High five:** two chibi hands slapping a high five in the middle, with a bright star burst.
12. **Hug:** two small chibi mascots hugging each other warmly (one in a warm-orange scarf, one in a sky-blue scarf so they can be told apart), with a small heart.
13. **Pair pose:** two small chibi mascots side by side making V-signs for a photo, with a camera-flash sparkle.
14. **Photo mode:** a cute chunky vintage camera with a round lens and a bright flash burst. No face needed.
