# REFUGEE — Story illustrations (29 full-screen scenes)

You are painting **story illustrations (event CGs)** for **REFUGEE (ค่ายผู้อพยพ)**, a fantasy RPG played on phones. Another assistant (Claude) will put each picture into the game. Each one fades in **full screen** during the story cutscene at that moment, while the dialogue box plays over the bottom.

So far the cutscenes show only small in-game sprites standing and talking. These paintings are the big emotional moments of the 10-chapter story. Please make them feel like **key art from an anime fantasy film**.

## Format: every image
- **One image per scene**, **1536×1024 landscape**, PNG. Name the files `cg_01.png … cg_29.png` exactly as numbered below.
- **Full-bleed illustration**: no text, no letters, no logo, no signature, no watermark, no UI, no frame, no black letterbox bars.
- **Safe area** (see `template_safe_area.png`): on wide phone screens the **top ~170 px and bottom ~170 px may be cut off**. Keep every face and the key action inside the middle band, with the main subject near the centre. The top and bottom strips should be sky, ground or atmosphere only.
- The bottom part of the screen will have a semi-transparent dialogue box over it, so **no important faces in the bottom quarter**.

## Style: match `style/approved_style_game_cover.jpg`
- This is the approved cover art of the game. **Match its look**: rich painterly anime fantasy, glowing magic light, deep blue-violet night skies with warm gold and amber accents, atmospheric haze, fireflies and sparkles, cinematic depth.
- Characters use **normal anime proportions** (not chibi), with expressive faces and cinematic lighting.
- Each scene has **one clear focal point** and a strong mood (awe, dread, warmth, sorrow, triumph). Use a different camera angle for each scene: wide establishing shots, low angles under giant beings, over-the-shoulder shots, close-ups of hands.
- **The same style in all 29 images**, so they read as one set.

## The hero: the most important rule
The hero is **the player's own character**. Players choose their own gender, face, class and outfit, so **never show the hero's face** and never make the hero clearly male or female.
- Show the hero **from behind, from over the shoulder, as a distant silhouette, or as just a hand**.
- Always the same look: a **lone traveler in a dark hooded travel cloak** (deep teal-brown, worn edges, hood up), with a small backpack and no visible weapon.
- **The Mark**: a **glowing golden circular sigil on the palm of the right hand** (a sun-like ring of runes). It is the hero's symbol and appears in many scenes.

## World summary (for context)
300 years ago the kingdom of **Aranya** sealed a god of light beneath the earth with a **Spirit Tower of 100 floors** and the souls of 100 knights. The hero wakes up at a **refugee camp** (wooden palisade, canvas tents, campfires, lanterns, forest) with a mysterious glowing mark on the palm. Later chapters go up to the **floating city Astra** (white-gold palaces on an island above a sea of clouds, held up by a giant glowing "Star Heart"), across a **sea of clouds** full of sky ships, up to **the stars**, and finally down **beneath the earth**. The central theme is **opening doors**: refugees nobody wanted end up connecting every city, so that no one has to carry the world alone.

## The 29 scenes
Paint them **in order**. If you can only do part of the list, each image is useful on its own.

### Chapter 1: The Broken Seal
**cg_01. The ancient seal (legend, 300 years ago).** Painted like an old legend: warm sepia and faded gold, like an old tapestry or fresco. Robed mages of the kingdom of Aranya and a hundred armoured knights stand in a vast ring around a hole in the earth. Their souls rise as blue lights into a **colossal 100-floor stone tower** being sealed over the pit. Golden chains of light run down into the ground. Beneath, through cracks, glows a huge sleeping golden presence.

**cg_02. Waking at the camp gate.** Night, poison-green mist. The hero lies half-raised on the ground outside the **wooden gate of the refugee camp**, seen from behind and above, among the shadowy carcasses of defeated monsters. The right palm glows with **the Mark**. **Commander Renn** stands at the gate holding a torch, looking down in surprise. Behind him, refugees peek from between the palisade logs. Watchtower lanterns glow.

**cg_03. The voice beneath the seal (top of the Spirit Tower).** The hero, tiny, stands at the very top of the Spirit Tower at night, seen from behind, right hand raised with **the Mark** blazing. In the sky in front, **two enormous golden eyes** of a god open through the clouds, and beneath them a colossal shape of golden light is bound by **a hundred glowing chains** that run down into the tower. Awe, not horror: the god is sad and ancient.

**cg_04. Ending "Seal": eternal seal.** A **pillar of icy-blue light** flows from the hero's palm into the Spirit Tower. The golden eyes in the sky slowly close. The poison mist across the land clears in waves, and dawn breaks over the refugee camp below. Calm, bittersweet.

**cg_05. Ending "Release": the god unchained.** The hundred chains **shatter into golden shards**. A **pillar of golden light** bursts from the top of the Spirit Tower into the sky, and a radiant winged silhouette of light rises inside it. The hooded **Tower Warden** kneels in tears at the edge of the tower top. The hero stands in the wind, cloak flying.

### Chapter 2: The Broken Sky
**cg_06. A crack in the sky.** Night over the refugee camp. **A jagged black crack** splits the starry sky, leaking violet light, and it widens. Far away above the clouds, the **floating city Astra** glows white and gold on its flying island, lifted by a giant glowing Star Heart underneath. Refugees around a campfire point up. The hero is seen from behind on a hill.

**cg_07. The queen's thanks.** On a high palace balcony of Astra at night, **Queen Elira** turns to the hero (seen from behind, hood up) with a warm, grateful smile. Below, the whole floating city celebrates with **fireworks** and lanterns, and the restored stars fill the sky.

### Chapter 3: The Shadow Land Beneath the Clouds
**cg_08. Shadows climbing the walls.** Night in Astra. **Black shadow-vines** rise from the sea of clouds below the island and creep up the white city walls like ivy. In the foreground, **Queen Elira** hands the hero an old **silver key** at a huge, sealed, cloud-covered **Cloud Gate** carved with stars. **Silas** the astrologer watches, worried.

**cg_09. The Cloud Gate opens.** The great Cloud Gate stands open. A **shaft of warm starlight** pours down from Astra through the parting clouds onto a dark, forgotten land far below, where ruined towers and faint figures look up into the light for the first time in 300 years. Hopeful, a promise kept.

### Chapter 4: The Divided Heart
**cg_10. Black roots at midnight.** The **Spirit Tower** at midnight. The ground around it splits open in long cracks and **huge black roots** erupt and coil around the tower's base. The golden light at the top of the tower has gone out. Commander Renn and frightened refugees hold torches at a distance. The hero's palm burns gold.

**cg_11. Half a heart.** Deep in the glowing **World Core** (molten amber light, giant ancient roots), the **Root Devourer** (see `bosses/root_devourer`, huge) crumbles into millions of dry roots. **Half of a glowing golden heart** floats in front of the hero's outstretched hand. The translucent blue ghost knight **Aldric** stands beside, solemn. A moment of decision.

### Chapter 5: The Floating Throne
**cg_12. Ambush in the throne hall.** The grand golden **throne hall of Astra**: nobles in two rows, chandeliers. In one instant everything turns cold: **all the light becomes icy silver**, and silver light-tendrils coil around **Queen Elira** and drag her down into the floor beneath the throne, while her hand reaches out. Royal guards turn their spears toward the hero, **eyes blank glowing silver**. **Liria** grabs the hero's arm from behind a curtain.

**cg_13. Every gate opens.** Dawn. All the gates of the floating city Astra swing open at once, and a **wide bridge of golden light** descends in a long curve from the city down to the little **refugee camp** far below. **Queen Elira** stands at the top with **Cassian** and the Star Guard. **Liria** sings, and crowds of citizens line the walls singing with her. The hero's Mark glows warm gold.

### Chapter 6: The Dawn Bridge
**cg_14. The first caravan.** Early morning on the **Dawn Bridge** (a bridge of light from Astra down to the camp). The first caravan of **200 refugees** walks up: families, old people, children, carts, bundles, a goat. **Cassian** and the Star Guard salute at the bridge head. Far below the bridge, a **dark shadow** slides along its edge back into the clouds.

**cg_15. The edge of the Heart Pit.** A huge circular pit with spiral stone stairs and great broken gears (see `places/empty_heart_pit_props`). On the stairs stand **thousands of shadow people**, quiet and waiting. At the rim, facing them, stand **Queen Elira**, **Cassian**, **Commander Renn**, a group of camp people and **Vesper**. The hero stands at the centre between both groups, seen from behind. Tense, hopeful silence.

### Chapter 7: The Gatherer of Fallen Stars
**cg_16. A rain of falling stars.** Night above the refugee camp. **Hundreds of shooting stars** streak across the sky and fall glowing onto the plain to the east. In the foreground **Til** runs toward the hero, out of breath and grinning, holding up a round **bronze star compass** that glows. Camp people look up.

**cg_17. The lullaby of three cities.** A warm night at the glowing **Heart Forge**, a great new heart of light in the camp. **Grandma Eda** sits singing with her eyes closed, a shawl over her knees. Around her, **Til**, **Celeste** (rolling up a star map) and people from three cities sit together, some singing along. Gentle, tearful, home.

### Chapter 8: The Endless Sea of Clouds
**cg_18. Lanterns and a fallen ship.** Night at the old **cloud harbor cliff** (see `places/cloud_harbor_buildings`). Above the sea of clouds float **hundreds of lanterns**, blinking in rhythm. A great **sky ship has crashed** against the cliff and is burning, with red smoke. In the clouds around it, the shadows of **enormous fish** swim. **Til** points, the hero beside him.

**cg_19. The whale rises.** The sea of clouds splits in two. The **Sky-Swallowing Whale** (see `bosses/sky_swallowing_whale`, but **the size of an island**, dark violet with constellation markings) rises with one eye bigger than a ship. Its mouth opens wide over a small boat where the boy **Navin** looks up. **Captain Grif** holds the hero back at the cloud edge. Overwhelming scale.

**cg_20. A home for the cloud fleet.** One month later, a full-moon night at the rebuilt **cloud harbor**, now a real home. People lift lanterns of light into the sky, and the great whale, now gentle ("Grandma Whale"), swims above the harbor in the moonlight. **Navin** stands on the pier with his two compasses. **Captain Grif** teaches children to tie ship knots. **Captain Sara** and **Grandma Mekha** watch, smiling.

### Chapter 9: The Endless Starfield
**cg_21. The stair to the stars.** On top of **Orion's observatory** at night, **Celeste** steps back from a giant brass telescope and **Orry** the brass robot points upward. The stars are being **pulled upward** in streaks toward one dark point high above, and from beneath the observatory a **stairway of light** opens and rises into space.

**cg_22. The field of star-webs.** Out in space: a vast **web of glowing silver threads** stretches across the cosmos, and **captured stars hang on every thread like dewdrops**. At the far centre there is a dark cocoon, and beyond it the shape of **the Weaver** (see `bosses/the_weaver`) at its loom. The hero and **Lumina** (flying, glowing) walk carefully along one thread toward it.

**cg_23. Every star returned.** The night sky is full again, brighter than ever. A **silver bridge of stars** arches across the whole sky. At the top of the observatory, **Orry** counts the stars, and **Celeste** writes the last page in her old master's notebook. Far below, tiny people walk up the star bridge. Peaceful and triumphant.

### Chapter 10: The Land That Carries the Sky (final chapter)
**cg_24. The land sinks.** Morning after a midnight earthquake. The **refugee camp** has sunk: the flagpole leans, and the southern field has collapsed into a **huge abyss**. At its edge a **red flag** marks the way down, and deep inside the abyss **a warm golden glow** shines like hundreds of lanterns. **Commander Renn**, muddy to the knees, and **Til** stand at the edge with the hero.

**cg_25. The First Bearer.** Beneath the earth there is no ground, only a bottomless darkness crossed by a gigantic **net of roots**. At the end of the net, the colossal stone giant **Thoranin** (see `characters/thoranin_first_bearer_stone_giant`) **kneels on the last rock of the world**, both arms raised, **holding up the base of the land above his head**. Thousands of roots wrap his arms and shoulders to help him carry it. His shins are covered in glowing red bite marks. **Thuli** lifts her mushroom lantern toward him. The hero is tiny on a root bridge. Epic scale.

**cg_26. Everyone came down.** Out of the darkness above, **people from every city climb down five long ropes** into the root net, holding lanterns, shovels, spears and woven cloth, until the net glows with a long trail of lights. **The Star Guard** with silver spears, **Captain Grif** shouting orders from a basket, and **Commander Renn** with a torn cloak jumping down from the last rope. **Thoranin** in the background, eyes closed, exhausted. The most hopeful image of the game.

**cg_27. Ending A: the home whose doors never close.** Months later. **Nine new giant trees** of different kinds (glass-star, sun-gear, cloud-salt) send roots down to help **Thoranin**, who **stands straight for the first time** in 300 years. In the foreground, root-city people climb a **staircase of roots** up into the open air and see the starry sky for the first time. **Thuli** leads them and **blows out her mushroom lantern**, smiling: there are stars up here.

**cg_28. Ending B: the home with the deepest roots.** Beneath the earth, a great **golden root** has grown down from a new tree in the refugee camp and wraps gently around **Thoranin's shoulders**, taking the weight. The stone giant has lowered his arms onto it and **sleeps for the first time in 300 years**. Tiny **Thuli** whispers into his huge ear. Warm golden light, deep peace.

**cg_29. Welcome home (epilogue).** Evening at the **refugee camp**, now a thriving town. The campfire in the centre, **all the gates open**, flags of eight cities on the fence, a new glowing tree beside the healed crack where children from two cities swing on one branch. **Commander Renn** stands by the fire, smiling, holding an open ledger of refugee names with a quill. The hero stands opposite him, seen from behind. Golden hour, the warmest image of the set.
